Short version: hack the challenges, not the platform or each other, and keep your flags inside your team.

## At a glance

- **Format:** Jeopardy: pick any challenge, in any order
- **Brackets:** Students and Open
- **Flag format:** `flag{...}`
- **Length:** 48 hours

## Teams

- A team has 1 to 4 players. Each player can be on one team only.
- The Students bracket is for teams where every player is enrolled in a school or university. Organizers may ask for proof.
- You can change your roster until the event starts. After that, players can leave but nobody new can join.

## Flags

Every flag looks like `flag{some_text_here}` unless the challenge says otherwise. Flags are case-sensitive. Submit the whole thing, braces included.

You can submit 10 flags per minute per team. Wrong guesses don't cost points.

You read this far, so here is the Sanity Check flag: `flag{read_the_rules}`

## Scoring

Every challenge is worth a fixed number of points, set by its difficulty: Easy challenges are worth 50 to 200, Medium 250 to 400, and Hard 450 to 500. Solving a challenge first earns first blood on its page but no extra points.

When two teams have the same score, the team that reached it first ranks higher.

## Hints

Some challenges have hints. Unlocking one costs 25 points, even if you never solve the challenge. Each hint unlocks for the whole team.

## What is and isn't allowed

### Allowed

- Attacking challenge servers in the ways the challenge invites
- Any tools, scripts or AI assistants you like
- Asking organizers for help if a challenge looks broken
- Writing up solutions after the event ends

### Not allowed

- Attacking the platform, scoreboard or other teams
- Sharing flags or solutions with other teams during the event
- Brute forcing the flag form
- Heavy automated scanning, unless a challenge says to

Breaking these rules can get your team removed from the scoreboard. Organizers make the final call.

## Scoreboard freeze

The public scoreboard freezes 2 hours before the end. You can still submit flags and see your own score. Final results are revealed at the closing stream.
